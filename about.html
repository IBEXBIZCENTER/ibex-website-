Who we are

IBEX Business Center is your trusted local destination for shipping, mailbox rentals, notary services, printing, and passport photos. We proudly serve residents, small businesses, and entrepreneurs throughout Dumfries with professional, in-person support and fast turnaround.

What sets us apart is how we treat people. Instead of automated phone trees or long waits, our team gives you hands-on help so every visit is simple and stress-free — whether you're shipping a package, renting a mailbox, or getting a document notarized.

From secure mail handling to reliable shipping through USPS, UPS, and FedEx, we're here to support your personal and business needs with efficiency, accuracy, and convenience.
